Section 02

Three ways to book — one shared brain

Patients can start a booking from the website, an on-site chat assistant, or WhatsApp directly. Whichever they choose, it checks the same live availability and lands in the same place for you.

1 · Book Online (website form)

A guided pop-up: in-clinic or online visit → treatment → doctor → live date & time slots pulled straight from the clinic calendar → patient details → secure payment → confirmation screen.

2 · The AI chat assistant

A chat bubble in the corner of every page. Walks the same booking steps conversationally, answers general questions ("do you do Invisalign?") using AI without derailing an in-progress booking, and can look up or cancel an existing appointment by its code.

3 · WhatsApp booking bot

Message the clinic's WhatsApp number and the same booking flow runs entirely inside WhatsApp using tap-to-choose menus (no typing required) — treatment category, then treatment, then date, then morning/evening, then an exact time slot. A payment link is sent directly in the chat. Every confirmed appointment gets two tappable buttons in WhatsApp: Reschedule and Cancel Appointment — no codes to remember or type back in.

Why this matters: a slot booked through WhatsApp is instantly unavailable on the website and vice versa — there's one live source of truth, so double-booking isn't possible regardless of which door the patient walked in through.
Any channel → Live slot check → Payment → Confirmed on Calendar → Logged for you

Section 03

Payments

A small refundable advance secures every slot, collected securely through Razorpay — India's standard payment gateway for clinics and hospitals.

  • Card, UPI, netbanking, wallets — all handled by Razorpay's own secure checkout screen; your website never sees or stores card details.
  • Separate fee for in-clinic vs. online consultations — both amounts are yours to set and change any time from the admin console.
  • Automatic confirmation — the moment a payment succeeds, the appointment is confirmed, added to the calendar, and logged, with no manual step for you.
  • Failed payments let the patient retry instead of dead-ending — on the website, in the chat assistant, and on WhatsApp.
  • Treatment price ranges shown on the site are informational only — the only amount ever actually charged, on any channel, is the advance booking fee you configure.

Section 04

Calendar & video consultations

Your Google Calendar is the single real record of every appointment — the website reads live availability from it and writes every confirmed booking back to it automatically.

Live availability

Time slots shown to patients reflect your actual calendar in real time — anything already booked or blocked off disappears from the options instantly.

Automatic invites

Confirmed appointments are added to your calendar with the patient's details, and an invite is emailed to them where available.

Video consultations wait for your OK

A Google Meet link is never generated automatically the moment someone books an online consultation — you may not actually be free at the slot they picked. Instead it appears in your admin console as "awaiting approval." One tap either confirms it (which creates the Meet link and sends it to the patient on WhatsApp) or releases the slot so they can pick another time. No patient ever receives a video link for a slot you haven't actually agreed to.

Section 05

Patient records & the appointment log

Two quiet systems run behind every booking so nothing is ever lost, even if the website's server restarts.

WhatWhat it's for
Patient databaseEvery patient is recorded once and matched by phone or email across all three booking channels, so returning patients don't create duplicate records no matter how they book.
Appointment spreadsheetA plain, human-readable log of every booking — patient, service, date, time, channel, and payment status — that you or your staff can open directly, no technical knowledge needed.
Appointment memoryThe booking system remembers every appointment permanently, so restarting or updating the website never wipes today's or tomorrow's bookings.

Section 06

Your admin console

A private, PIN-protected page only you have the link to — not listed anywhere on the public site — where you control the parts of the website that change over time.

Booking advance fees

Set the in-clinic and online consultation advance amounts, or switch the fee off entirely for free bookings.

Treatment price ranges

Edit the displayed price range for any of the 13 treatments — updates the website and the WhatsApp bot instantly.

Pending online consults

Approve or release video-consultation requests, as described in Section 04.

Blog publishing

Write, edit, or remove blog posts — an image, a title, the content, and an author is all that's needed. Goes live on the public Blog page immediately.

Google review count

Shown live from Google automatically, or set the number yourself at any time if you'd rather control it manually.

Calendar connection

A one-click way to (re)connect the website to a Google account — used once now, and again only if the clinic's Google account ever changes.

Section 07

Getting found on Google

The site is written and structured to be found by people actually searching for a dentist nearby — not just to look good once they arrive.

Local search Google rich results Voice & AI search Social sharing
  • Region-specific keywords throughout — Kalapatti, Coimbatore, and the surrounding neighbourhoods (Saravanampatti, Thudiyalur, Ganapathy, Vilankurichi), alongside every treatment name.
  • Structured business data submitted to Google — clinic hours, address, doctors and their specialities, so Google can show accurate details directly in search results and Maps.
  • FAQ rich-result eligibility — the "Common Patient Questions" on the Services page are marked up so Google can surface them directly in search, above the normal listing.
  • Clean link previews — sharing the site on WhatsApp, Facebook, or Twitter shows a proper title, description, and photo instead of a bare link.

Section 09

Security & safeguards

The parts patients never see, working to keep payments, data, and admin access safe.

  • Every payment is verified twice — once when the patient's browser reports success, and again independently when Razorpay itself confirms it, so a booking can never be falsely marked as paid.
  • The admin console is PIN-locked and not linked from anywhere on the public site — only reachable by someone who already has the direct address.
  • Repeated wrong PIN attempts are automatically slowed down, making guessing impractical.
  • Incoming WhatsApp messages are verified as genuinely from Meta before the bot acts on them, preventing spoofed messages from reaching the booking system.
  • Patient and payment data is stored with access restricted to the website's own secure backend only — never exposed to browsers directly.

Section 10

What's left before go-live

A short, honest list of the remaining steps between where things stand today and the site running fully live on your own domain.

  • Pointing your domain to the live website and switching payments from test mode to real transactions.
  • Final WhatsApp Business verification with Meta, so messages send from the clinic's permanent number.
  • Reconnecting Google Calendar to the clinic's own Google account (currently connected to a development account for testing).

Everything described in Sections 01–09 above is already built and working end-to-end — these three items are account-level switches, not open development work.
